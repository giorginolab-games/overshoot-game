Measured features

FeatureYouTarget
Targets are simulated with published conductance-based models plus a little recording noise: Hodgkin–Huxley 1952 (squid axon), Pospischil et al. 2008 (cortical and thalamic neurons), and ten Tusscher–Noble–Noble–Panfilov 2004 (human ventricle). The T-wave level couples 60 of those ventricular cells in a 1.2 cm transmural strand and computes a pseudo-ECG from the voltage gradients, as in Gima & Rudy 2002. Each channel is a Hodgkin–Huxley-type conductance, g·(gates)·(V − E). The slider sets the maximal conductance, which is proportional to channel density. In the cardiac levels intracellular Na⁺ and K⁺ are held at their 1 Hz steady state, and features are scored on the second paced beat.
